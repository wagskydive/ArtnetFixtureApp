# Privacy Policy for DMX Projector

**Last updated:** March 20, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **DMX Projector**. This application is designed to transform Android TV devices or projectors into DMX-capable lighting fixtures by receiving Art-Net data over a local network.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### a. Personal Data

**DMX Projector does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information.**

---

### b. Network Data

The app listens for Art-Net (DMX over IP) data on your **local network only**.

* No data is transmitted outside your local network
* No Art-Net or DMX data is stored
* No data is sent to external servers

---

### c. Device Information

The app may access limited device information strictly required for functionality, such as:

* Network state (to receive Art-Net data)
* Basic system performance data (for app stability)

This information is **not collected, stored, or transmitted externally**.

---

## 3. In-App Purchases

DMX Projector may offer premium features through in-app purchases.

* All payments are processed securely by Google Play
* We do not collect or store payment information

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on third-party services provided by Google for:

* App distribution
* Licensing
* In-app purchases

These services may collect data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

---

## 5. Data Sharing

We **do not sell, trade, or share user data** with third parties.

---

## 6. Security

We take reasonable measures to ensure the app operates securely within your local network environment.

## 7. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
